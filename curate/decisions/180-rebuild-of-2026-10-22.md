## Found during the build

`sources/afli/convert.R` printed 5,655 `error > 60 min` lines: `geo::geoconvert.1()` meets a stored position with 60
or more minutes and converts it anyway. Studied with the maintainer the same evening
(`$FISHYDATA/archive/2026-10-afli-minutes60/`, TODO 126): exactly 60 is a rounding without a carry, and 61-99 is
unexplained. The study widened to the logbooks' positions: what each haul end holds (a point and its precision, a
cell, or nothing) by source, app and period. Nothing in this build changed afli's positions.
