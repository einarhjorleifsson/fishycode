vessel-years that also keep a longer track were looked at, and placeholder positions are now flagged first
(`curate/corrections/ais_invalid_position.yml`). Vessels faster than 25 kn lose real track under either filter: 84
fishing vessels, all under 12 m, 8,433 real pings, steaming only (a per-vessel limit, or not flagging a leg the
vessel's own recorded speed confirms). The forward scan accepts the first ping after a gap of more than 4 h untested
(vid 2657, 2017); sparse tracks (vid 1186, 2023). On simulated tracks in the review package
(github.com/einarhjorleifsson/test), vmask erodes good track beside a burst moved along the heading (8 good pings
lost), and a burst within reach of the last good ping is partly kept. On ramb's `whacks1` (12 misplaced of 46 pings),
`"clean"` and `"sda"` flag the 12 and 4 good pings beside them, `"forward"` and `"sequential"` exactly the 12,
`"fwdbwd"` only 2 (the book's Pings chapter, 2026-10-20). A third fast-vessel option, from Ifremer's iapesca
(2026-10-09, not built): a run of 3+ legs over 25 kn whose two-ping legs are fast too is real running; in June 2023
it held 1,115 of the filter's 8,942 flags, recorded SOG > 20 kn on 90-98 %; it needs a speed cap (decision 078).

**From TODO 99** (measured 2026-10-20): the test's one ceiling, `KN_MAX` 25 kn, lets a slow vessel's whacks through.
On vid 1417, a 7-knot boat, the vessel's own ceiling (99.9th percentile of its at-sea reported speed + 3 kn: 15.3 kn)
flags 132 steps, 42 out and back; the typical one is a ping 3 km off the track reporting 0 kn mid-run. Over the whole
trail (vessels with 1,000+ at-sea pings a year): 132,380 steps under 25 kn but over the vessel's own ceiling, 8,196 of
them out and back, 400-800 vessels a year. A second witness: each ping's reported speed against the step's implied
speed (two pings both at rest yet far apart), with care, since a harbour-to-harbour run with no ping between rightly
reports 0 at both ends (most of vid 1417's 31). Positions shared exactly by many vessels are berths, not ghosts, but
for STK's `U` pings: 115 positions each used by 10 or more units hold 482,668 of them, regional port markers (decision
164). The maintainer's idea: a second pass within voyages for the tests that need one; the per-vessel ceiling and the
speed check need no voyage, so they can go in the first pass with no circularity. Scripts:
`$FISHYDATA/archive/2026-10-todo98/scripts/` (`fan1417.R`, `voyage_gaps_ceiling.R`).
