## What was chosen

Every answer was the recommendation (plan 081 §8):

- **a, the proposal** ("As proposed"): for each name its kind, the thing, its extent, its whole and parts, its forms and
  every record's outcome, in a table of its own (`place_name_proposal`), versioned; it changes the names only when he
  accepts, as his ledger row.
- **b, the order** ("By feature type"): feature class by feature class over all Iceland (the troughs and deeps, the
  moraines, the banks with their parts, the volcanic ridges, the depth model's outlines and the grounds), each reviewed
  region by region; not region by region with every source (plan 080).
- **c, the skippers' measure** ("Concentration"): the share of a name's own tows on a feature, two vessels or more; no
  distance inside an outline; along a line the corridor read from the alignment's fall.
- **d, the grammar** ("As proposed"): a name a root and an ending, the first table of endings (plan 081 §4.2) read region
  by region, his word over it; the skippers' "-dýpi" one name with the charts' "-djúp".
- **e, the grounds** ("As proposed"): a ground's footprint from the effort by gear, all years together; the effort never as
  boundaries between names.
- **f, a bank's outline** ("Yes, the east first"): the 200 m line seaward, the neighbouring deeps' thalwegs toward their
  fjords on the sides, the coast landward; built first on the east coast and measured before the rest.
- **g, LMÍ** ("As proposed"): the name only, its source traced; the 1,909 chart names sized by an LMÍ area sized instead
  by their outline when proposed, else by the charts' lettering.
- **h, timarit.is** ("As proposed"): the stored pages read for each step's names as it is taken up, Bjarni Sæmundsson's 938
  flagged Andvari paragraphs first; more fetched only at his word.
- **i, plan 080** ("As proposed"): its forward design (§3, §5.3-§5.6, versions by region) superseded by plan 081; its
  versions 1 and 2 as built and its §4 rules kept. Plan 080 is executed.
- **j, the review** ("A mode on the page"): a review mode on the names page (port 8822), the proposal drawn beside every
  record, with accept, move or pin, another candidate, link, part of, rename and ignore a record; the flagged first.
