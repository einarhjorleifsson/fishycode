# 335 — Trackwell's names are public: they may stand in this repo, `overrides/` included

*2026-11-19. At the maintainer's word: "Treat all trackwell stuff public"; and "that include the data from the gps trails -
that is the messy part", then "i think", then "specifically the tracks with the names". Recorded by Claude (AI).*

## What was decided

Until now Trackwell's place names were held internal with the plotters' and the gazetteer's: never in this repo, which is
public on GitHub, not in `overrides/`, plans, TODO, decisions or commit messages; a ledger of verdicts on them was to live
under `$FISHYDATA` (the named-features study's practice since 2026-11-09, plan 080 §5.4 as first revised). The maintainer's
word lifts that for Trackwell: its names, and what is said of them, may stand in this repo like the charts', LMÍ's and ÍSOR's,
and the maintainer's verdicts on Trackwell's names go in the page's ledger in `overrides/`. It covers the tracks that carry a
name too (his word: "specifically the tracks with the names"): Trackwell's tows each with the place name its skipper gave, and
what is drawn from them (a tow's position, a name's use on a mound, counts) may be written here. Trackwell's trails beyond
those are not part of this decision. They are the messy part, in his word: the names come with tows that run along the flanks (plan 080 §2).

Why it came up: LMÍ republishes 121 of Trackwell's names in IS 50V (its delivery V202005003, 2020), so the same names were
public there while held internal here; on the Kolbeinsey Ridge they are the only names at a mound's scale (plan 080 §2).

## What it does not change

- The plotters' marks, the internal wreck lists and the gazetteer's other internal rows stay internal: under `$FISHYDATA`,
  never in this repo.
- Data stay under `$FISHYDATA`, as every dataset does, the named tracks among them; what changes is what may be written of
  them here. Handing the vessel-level trails themselves outside MFRI is a data release on MFRI's own terms (its gagnastefna),
  the maintainer's to arrange.
- The older records that name a Trackwell name (decisions 222 and 224, Gígurinn) need no change.

## Where it applies

Plan 080 §2 and §5.4 (Trackwell's names as candidates, the maintainer's verdicts in `overrides/`), and the memory rule that
had kept them out.
